How it works

A simple civic path.

Designed to make the official record easier to reach, without hiding uncertainty or provenance.

You enter your location.

Start from a city, postal code or location permission.

CitizenEye identifies your constituency.

Geographic and administrative data are used to resolve the likely constituency.

Your representative is displayed.

The app shows the député associated with the confirmed place.

You explore public votes.

Recent votes and recorded positions become readable on mobile.

Sources stay visible.

Every piece of information can link back to official material.

Political neutrality

Facts before opinions

CitizenEye does not score politicians. CitizenEye does not rank representatives. CitizenEye does not generate outrage.

The goal is to make public information easier to access and verify.
